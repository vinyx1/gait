Limits you should know about

  • Data: TTC schedule file and City of Toronto open data (street furniture, road and sidewalk programs). Boarding flags come from the schedule file as published.
  • In this feed, every streetcar trip is flagged "not accessible" while buses and subway trips are mostly flagged accessible. The file does not explain this, and the TTC may describe accessibility differently. Check the TTC before relying on it.
  • "No shelter or bench listed" means the City dataset does not list one. It may be incomplete.
  • Walking uses straight-line distance times {{ snap.detour_factor }}. Curbs, slopes, snow, crossings and sidewalk condition were not checked.
  • Only the base weekday, Saturday and Sunday schedules are counted.
