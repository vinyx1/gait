    Limits you should know about

    • The route part of your trip follows the line the vehicle runs in the TTC schedule file. Walking parts are straight lines multiplied by {{ 1.3 }}. Real sidewalks, curbs, slopes, crossings and snow were not checked.
    • This does not time or plan a trip, and it cannot compare your trip before and after a change. The return trip is not checked.
    • Only decisions that this tool has loaded and located can appear. A city pilot would load every agenda item.
    • In the TTC file every streetcar trip is flagged "not accessible" while most buses and subway trips are flagged accessible. The file does not say why, and the TTC may describe accessibility differently. Check with the TTC.
    • Works lists come from City open data and can be out of date. Shelter and bench listings may be incomplete. Only the base weekday, Saturday and Sunday schedules are counted.
    • Rider walking limits are illustrative assumptions, not medical or legal definitions.
