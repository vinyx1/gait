# Landing page

Scope: `/` landing page. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: Toronto seniors (often on a tablet, reading glasses), checking how city decisions changed their bus trip; must need no outside help. Judges see it tomorrow.
Action: one obvious button, "Check my bus trip", to /map.
Proof: real computed trip (7 Bathurst, 62 m to 419 m for a senior with a walker), real 311 count, a real staff-report quote.
Avoid: childish, small or busy, alarming.
Keep: the turning-earth scroll concept and every fact.

## Direction contract

THESIS: City decisions told at the kitchen table where seniors read the paper; the turning earth is a round harvest-gold platter of the neighbourhood that turns like a lazy Susan, one scene per scroll. Refuses the category's white civic page with a headline, button and three feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: 1970s Toronto home kitchen. Whole-screen fields of harvest gold, avocado, burnt orange, walnut and Pyrex turquoise, one per scene, with dark brown or cream ink chosen for contrast. Flat stoneware-like drawings (rounded houses, trees, bus) on a walnut platter with an avocado rim and a dotted stoneware band. Young Serif display, Atkinson Hyperlegible body at 20px and up. Chunky pill controls that invert when pressed.

STORY: The visitor sees her walk to the bus was short, the stop was removed, the walk is now longer than her limit, winter makes waiting harder, every fact has a page number, and she can check her own trip.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Harvest-gold field. Left: wordmark, a two-line headline at about 72px, one sentence at 22px, and a dark brown 64px-tall button "Check my bus trip". Bottom-centre-right: the platter rises from the bottom edge, its lower half off screen, the city standing on its rim, a cream bus driving round.
